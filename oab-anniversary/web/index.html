A FEW THINGS TO KNOW

A gift for you.
A helping hand for them.

Who can use this offer?+

Previous OAB clients and their friends and family in the DFW area. All 3 referral consultations must be scheduled by December 31, 2026, to qualify. The past client earns their thank-you once all 3 visits are completed.

What do past clients get?+

When 3 people you refer each complete a free in-person consultation, choose a $150 Amazon gift card or $150 off your next home organizing order of $1,200 or more. When we organize your home, we include free donation drop-off and order your home organizing products for you. You just pay for the bins and products you choose.

What do new clients get?+

Your friends and family get a free in-person consultation and $150 off a home organizing order of $1,200 or more. We include donation drop-off and order their home organizing products for them. They just pay for the bins and products they choose.

What is the deadline?+

All 3 visits must be scheduled by December 31, 2026. You earn your thank-you after all 3 people complete their visits. They do not need to book a paid service for you to earn it.

How will you know I sent them?+

Choose your name from the list. We’ll add your referral code to your text and your friend’s link. When your friend fills out the claim form, the code is saved with their lead in our tracker. Our team confirms your thank-you once all 3 free visits are done.

How do I check my status?+

Text us your name and referral code. We’ll check the lead tracker and tell you how many free visits are left before you earn your thank-you.

Text us for my status ↗
Do I need to clean up or buy bins first?+

No. Show us the room as it is. We help you sort what stays and what goes. We can measure and order bins for you. You see the price first. You pay for the bins.

Which rooms can you help with?+

Closets, pantries, kitchens, garages, playrooms, home offices, laundry rooms, and more. Tell us which space needs help. We’ll talk through the job with you.

How much will my job cost?+

The price depends on your space and what you need. We give you a price before you book. The $150 discount applies to home organizing orders of $1,200 or more. We’ll also check which dates are open.
